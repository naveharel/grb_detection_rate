Settings
Optical survey mode

Two-timescale night schedule: each field is revisited every tcad = n × 1 day (the only valid optical cadences), with Nv visits at spacing Δtv inside each visit-night of length tnight. Activates tnight, Nv, Δtv; the exposure budget becomes feff·fnight·tcad/(Nexp·Nv) − tOH, and detection counts ≥ i visits inside the detectable window (intra-night pairs and multi-night accumulation both included).

Color by detection regime

Tints the surface by dominant regime: warm = flux-limited (DEuc), cool = cadence-limited (Di), gray = singly-limited (Ddec). Brighter = higher range.

Exact rate mode

Integrates Rdet over all viewing angles q ∈ [0, qnr] (thesis Eq. 39) instead of the dominant-term approximation — slower, but exact. This is the calibrated reference mode (the model-vs-ZTF validation numbers are computed in it with all refinements on); the default closed-form mode is a fast approximation — at Nv ≥ 2 it is the 5-point gap-average of per-channel rectangles. Unlocks the refinements below; the first time it is switched on they all turn on together, and each can then be toggled independently.

Include t− ≈ ton

Measures the detectability window from the light-curve turn-on ton ≈ tp (window start t− ≈ ton) instead of from the burst, so off-axis events must stay above the limit for the full required duration after they peak (the from-burst criterion credits detections to the pre-peak interval, where the off-axis flux is negligible). At Nv = 1 the required duration is the window convention below; at Nv ≥ 2 the schedule's phase ramp Pi(T) is integrated exactly, so the window-convention bracket is resolved rather than chosen. Suppresses the marginal off-axis population and moves the detected q-median on-axis.

Rise cut (srise): random start time

Applies the srise cut as a uniform random observation-start survival weight (only a fraction of bursts pass, depending on when the survey happens to catch them). Off: the best-case hard boundary (first detection assumed at the peak, as in the dominant-term mode), which raises the rate. No effect when srise = 0.

Fade cut (sfade): random start time

Applies the sfade cut as a uniform random observation-start survival weight. Off: the best-case hard boundary, which raises the rate. No effect when sfade = 0.

Other options
tOH approximation

Drops tOH from the exposure formula to enable closed-form optimal strategies, while still enforcing texp > tOH as the validity boundary.

Window spans (i−1)·tcad

Counts the required detectability duration as the i−1 gaps between the i detections (default) instead of adding one extra waiting gap (i·tcad, the conservative worst-case-wait convention — switch off for it). Enters wherever a hard window is used: qi, Di at Nv = 1 and in non-optical mode, and the per-channel exact-mode rectangles at Nv ≥ 2 (span Sc vs Sc + gc). No effect where the waiting time is integrated instead of bracketed: the closed-form gap-averaged mixture (Nv ≥ 2) and Exact rate mode with “Include t− ≈ ton” (the phase ramp resolves the bracket).
